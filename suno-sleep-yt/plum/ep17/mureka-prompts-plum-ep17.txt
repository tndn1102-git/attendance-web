【STYLE】 ★987자 (상한 1000)
Soul pop for a cafe, E flat minor to G flat major, 90 BPM. Female low alto, half voice,
near speech, as if humming to herself, behind the beat. Narrow melody in a fourth, one
phrase returning; the chorus keeps those notes and lifts because the band opens, not
because she pushes; lines end early. Rounded consonants, almost no vibrato. Full band,
dense and soft: dark tremolo Rhodes, three-note motif, struck short; muted piano, short
off-beat chords; one harmony voice, last chorus, no choir; low tape bass, short; rim
click, soft kick, dark shaker every bar, never dropping out. Harmony Ebm9 - Abm9 - Fm7b5
- Bb7b9 to Ebm9, ending on Gbmaj9. Vocals behind the band, blended in, not upfront. Dark
warm tone, highs rolled off, lo-fi. Ends on a plucked chord, about two and a half
minutes, the lyric filling all of it. One note per syllable, the band carrying every
lift. No melisma, no sustained high notes, no head voice, no saxophone, no smoky bar or
lounge, no autotune, no pop gloss.

【TITLE】
a minute tastes longer here
