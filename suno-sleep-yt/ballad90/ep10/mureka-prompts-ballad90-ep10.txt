【STYLE】 ★975자
Early-2000s Korean bright band pop, sung in Korean by a female mezzo, C4 to E4, fresh and quick, no male vocals, never belted, never strained, no rasp. G major, 118 BPM, driving but light. A jangly clean electric guitar strums the pulse and leads, a second clean guitar answering in short rising figures. A real kit played light: closed hi-hat in eighths, snare on the backbeat, handclaps on two and four, one tom fill before the last chorus. The pre-chorus climbs stepwise into a lifted add-nine chord, the one bright event. Quick smiling delivery, slightly behind the beat, small vibrato at line ends only. The band is fuller in the last chorus, not the voice, no crescendo. The backing track has a dark top end with no cymbal sizzle. The voice keeps its natural breath and air, close-miked and present. Lead vocal clearly in front of the band. No English lyrics, no autotune, no trot, no K-pop gloss, no distorted guitar, no guitar solo. Bright airy modern mix, not retro.

    8분햇+핸드클랩(5·7) · 삼바 브러시+림(6). 무햇 곡 0곡.
